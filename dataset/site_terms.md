# Site terms read for each source

The terms of the three sources of dataset version 2.0.0 were read again on Nov 23, 2026 (Pacific). The first reading of all five sources was on Nov 4, 2026 (UTC), when every page was fetched by script and the labels were proposed by a language model.

## The three sources that remain

Microsoft Azure: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/terms-of-use , last updated February 7, 2022. "Unless otherwise specified, the Services are for your personal and non-commercial use." Its restriction on scraping applies to Microsoft's AI services, not to the status history. No clause on linking to the site; quoting for research not addressed.

Google Cloud: https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=en , effective July 30, 2026. Automated access is barred only against machine-readable instructions, and the robots.txt of status.cloud.google.com allows every agent. "You may not copy, modify, distribute, sell, or lease any part of our services" (aimed at services and software); linking not addressed.

GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service , effective April 27, 2026. Section D.9 access limits "does not apply to Access solely for the purpose of academic research". GitHub's Acceptable Use Policies, section 7, allow research use of public, non-personal information when the resulting publication is open access. Linking and short quotes are not restricted.

## The two sources withdrawn in version 2.0.0

AWS: https://aws.amazon.com/terms/ , Last Updated June 4, 2025. The licence the terms grant does not cover data mining, robots or similar data gathering and extraction tools. Its 18 reports were collected by script, so they were withdrawn.

Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/website-terms/ , effective August 1, 2025, section 8. Automated bots may not access, scan, scrape, data mine or copy the site to develop, train, fine-tune or improve an AI system, or for its operation, unless robots.txt allows that bot by name. Its 42 reports were collected by script and labelled with a language model, so they were withdrawn.

This is a record of what each source's terms say and why two sources were withdrawn. It is not a legal finding.

Only each report's link, its title, its date, labels and short notes that paraphrase the report are released. No report text is redistributed.
