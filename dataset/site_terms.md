# Site terms read for each source before collection (read 2026-11-04 UTC)

AWS: https://aws.amazon.com/terms/ , Last Updated June 4, 2025. Personal-use license; no commercial reproduction; linking grant covers only "a limited, revocable, and nonexclusive right to create a hyperlink to the home page" (deep links and short research quotes are not addressed).

Microsoft Azure: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/terms-of-use , last updated February 7, 2022. "Unless otherwise specified, the Services are for your personal and non-commercial use." No clause on linking to the site; quoting for research not addressed.

Google Cloud: https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=en , effective July 30, 2026. "You may not copy, modify, distribute, sell, or lease any part of our services" (aimed at services and software); linking not addressed; terms mention "fair use" exceptions to copyright.

Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/website-terms/ , effective August 1, 2025. Only bot restriction is for AI/ML use: "You may not use automated bots to access, scan, scrape, data mine, copy" (for training or improving AI systems); no clause on linking or short quotes.

GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service , effective April 27, 2026. Section D.9 access limits "does not apply to Access solely for the purpose of academic research"; linking and short quotes not restricted. The github.blog site was not checked for separate blog terms.

Only each report's link, its title, its date and our own labels are released. No report text is redistributed.
