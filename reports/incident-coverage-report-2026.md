# Incident Coverage Report 2026

How much of what breaks in public cloud incidents can today's open-source chaos tools inject?

Yogesh Ramaswamy, Independent Researcher. Generated by incident2chaos 0.1.0 from its released corpus; Chaos Mesh commit e636338c, LitmusChaos chaos-charts commit b2662c23.

## The answer

Of the 484 public post-incident reports that state their primary failure, 274 (56.6 percent, 95 percent interval 52.2 to 61.0) have a primary failure that Chaos Mesh or LitmusChaos can inject directly. 208 can only be approximated and 2 cannot be injected at all.

## How to reproduce

```bash
pip install git+https://github.com/yogeshramaswamy608/incident2chaos@v0.1.0
incident2chaos coverage --by source
```

The corpus holds each report's link and labels, never its text. The labels of the reports outside the 50-report pilot were proposed by a language model under the frozen codebook and checked by the author on a blind sample of 45 (injectability agreement 42 of 44, Cohen's kappa 0.91).
