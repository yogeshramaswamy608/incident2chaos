# Incident Coverage Report 2026

How much of what breaks in public cloud incidents can today's open-source chaos tools inject?

Yogesh Ramaswamy, Independent Researcher. Generated by incident2chaos 0.2.1 from its released corpus; Chaos Mesh commit e636338c, LitmusChaos chaos-charts commit b2662c23.

## The answer

Of the 429 public post-incident reports that state their primary failure, 199 (46.4 percent, 95 percent interval 41.7 to 51.1) have a primary failure that Chaos Mesh or LitmusChaos can inject directly. 228 can only be approximated and 2 cannot be injected at all.

## How to reproduce

```bash
pip install git+https://github.com/yogeshramaswamy608/incident2chaos@v0.2.1
incident2chaos coverage --by source
```

The corpus holds each report's link and labels, never its text. A language model proposed the labels of all 437 reports. The author checked the codebook's mapping of the 20 fault classes to the two catalogs and changed four (codebook version 3). The author checked the proposed labels of the 34-report pilot and changed 9. The labels of the other 403 reports, proposed under the frozen codebook, were not revised afterwards; the author coded a sample of 43 of them blind (injectability agreement 39 of 42, Cohen's kappa 0.86, under codebook version 3).
