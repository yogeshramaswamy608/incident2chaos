# Incident Coverage Report 2026

How much of what breaks in public cloud incidents can today's open-source chaos tools inject?

Yogesh Ramaswamy, Independent Researcher. Generated by incident2chaos 0.2.0 from its released corpus; Chaos Mesh commit e636338c, LitmusChaos chaos-charts commit b2662c23.

## The answer

Of the 429 public post-incident reports that state their primary failure, 240 (55.9 percent, 95 percent interval 51.2 to 60.6) have a primary failure that Chaos Mesh or LitmusChaos can inject directly. 187 can only be approximated and 2 cannot be injected at all.

## How to reproduce

```bash
pip install git+https://github.com/yogeshramaswamy608/incident2chaos@v0.2.0
incident2chaos coverage --by source
```

The corpus holds each report's link and labels, never its text. A language model proposed the labels of all 437 reports. The author checked the proposed labels of the 34-report pilot and changed 9. The labels of the other 403 reports, proposed under the frozen codebook, were not revised afterwards; the author coded a sample of 43 of them blind (injectability agreement 40 of 42, Cohen's kappa 0.91).
